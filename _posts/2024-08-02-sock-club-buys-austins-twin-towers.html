Sock Club Buys Austin's Twin Towers

August 2, 2024

The Real Deal covered Sock Club's purchase of the Twin Towers office building at 1106 Clayton Lane in Austin.

According to the report, Sock Club acquired the 190,600-square-foot property from Omninet Capital, with plans to move its offices into the building and welcome other emerging businesses as tenants. The five-story building dates to 1973 and occupies roughly seven acres near I-35 and Highway 290. The purchase price was not disclosed.

This is a summary of reporting by TRD Staff, published August 2, 2024.
